When to involve Novel Insights

Three situations I am usually called into

  • A consequential decision involves competing options, unclear dependencies and trade-offs nobody has made explicit.
  • There is strategic direction, but no coherent target architecture and no realistic sequence for getting there.
  • Several programmes, platforms and suppliers are moving at once, and their decisions have stopped adding up to a coherent whole.

How I help

Three ways to engage Novel Insights

Recommended starting point

Architecture Decision Support

For decisions that are expensive, contested or difficult to reverse. I compare the realistic options against explicit criteria, expose the dependencies and consequences behind each, and produce a recommendation that holds up under governance scrutiny.

What you receive: Options analysis, evaluation criteria, dependency and risk analysis, a recommended direction, and a decision-ready pack.

Target Architecture and Transformation Roadmap

For organisations that know where they want to go but not how the pieces should fit or in what order. I define the target architecture, the transition states between here and there, and the sequence that makes the change achievable.

What you receive: Current and target-state views, transition architectures, architecture principles, dependency analysis, implementation sequence and governance checkpoints.

Architecture Assurance and Advisory Support

For programmes already in motion, where coherence is at risk across projects, systems and suppliers. Independent senior review and advice, so that decisions stay aligned as they meet delivery reality.

What you receive: Design and conformance reviews, decision records, governance support, and agreed senior advisory involvement during delivery.

From the founder

Dr. Soufiane Bouarfa

Novel Insights was founded by Dr. Soufiane Bouarfa, an enterprise architect whose doctoral research at TU Delft examined how complex sociotechnical systems — specifically air transportation — remain safe and resilient under uncertainty. He found the same patterns inside large organisations: failures rarely come from one broken component, but from many locally sensible decisions that stop adding up to a coherent whole.

Novel Insights brings that perspective to governments and regulated organisations, combining research rigour with practical architecture. Vendor-independent, and senior throughout.
