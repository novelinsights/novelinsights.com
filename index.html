From the founder

Dr. Soufiane Bouarfa

Novel Insights was founded by Dr. Soufiane Bouarfa, an enterprise architect whose doctoral research at TU Delft examined how complex sociotechnical systems — specifically air transportation — remain safe and resilient under uncertainty. He found the same patterns inside large organisations: failures rarely come from one broken component, but from many locally sensible decisions that stop adding up to a coherent whole.

Novel Insights brings that perspective to governments and regulated organisations, combining research rigour with practical architecture. Vendor-independent, and senior throughout.
