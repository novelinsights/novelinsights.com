How I help

Three ways to engage Novel Insights

Recommended starting point

Architecture Decision Support

For decisions that are expensive, contested or difficult to reverse. I compare the realistic options against explicit criteria, expose the dependencies and consequences behind each, and produce a recommendation that holds up under governance scrutiny.

What you receive: An Architecture Decision Pack: options analysis, explicit evaluation criteria, dependencies, risks and trade-offs, a recommended direction, and a decision-ready presentation that a board or steering group can use directly.

Discuss a decision →

Target Architecture and Transformation Roadmap

For organizations that know where they want to go but not how the pieces should fit or in what order. I define the target architecture, the transition states between here and there, and the sequence that makes the change achievable.

What you receive: Current and target-state views, transition architectures, architecture principles, dependency analysis, implementation sequence and governance checkpoints.

Architecture Assurance and Advisory Support

For programs already in motion, where coherence is at risk across projects, systems and suppliers. Independent senior review and advice, so that decisions stay aligned as they meet delivery reality.

What you receive: Design and conformance reviews, decision records, governance support, and agreed senior advisory involvement during delivery.

Areas of knowledge

The subjects this work involves

Information and knowledge architecture
Connecting services, processes, information objects, systems and ownership, including the knowledge platforms through which that information is published and maintained, so the whole chain can be seen and governed.
Integration and platform transformation
Designing how platforms, suppliers and systems fit together, and how an organization moves between them without losing continuity.
Resilience and critical systems
Revealing how critical services depend on technology, information, people, suppliers and governance, and where continuity may fail.
Responsible AI and adaptive systems
Data foundations, lineage and ownership; monitoring for drift; and the accountability structures that keep human judgment in the loop when systems learn, adapt or automate consequential decisions.

Approach

A structured approach, built for complexity

01

Frame

Establish the real question, constraints, stakeholders and what a successful outcome would look like.

02

Map

Make the relationships visible: dependencies, interactions and the points where consequences propagate. Views and viewpoints are developed around the concerns of specific stakeholders, so that different parts of the organization can reason about the same system.

03

Structure

Develop realistic options with explicit trade-offs, then define the target picture, principles, governance and roadmap.

04

Support

Remain involved as architectural decisions meet delivery reality: reviews, workshops and advice, at the pace the delivery teams actually work.
