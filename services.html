Core services

Architecture that connects intent with execution

Enterprise Architecture Capability and Governance

Connecting strategy, capabilities, information and technology, and establishing the governance needed to maintain coherence as the organisation changes.

This includes improving the architecture function itself: principles, reference architectures, portfolio alignment, design authority, decision records and clear domain ownership.

What you receive: Capability model, target architecture, architecture principles, governance instruments and a sequenced roadmap.

Solution and Integration Architecture

Designing secure, scalable and compliant solutions that fit the wider architecture around them.

A technically sound solution must also connect correctly to the capabilities, processes, information and controls it is intended to support.

What you receive: Solution design, integration and data-flow views, security and compliance considerations, and documented decisions and trade-offs.

Information and Knowledge Architecture

Structuring information so that people and systems can find it, interpret it, govern it and reuse it consistently.

This includes information models, metadata, ownership, publication structures, knowledge platforms and the relationships between operational information and enterprise systems.

What you receive: Information model, metadata and ownership approach, knowledge or publication architecture, and an implementation roadmap.

Transformation Architecture and Roadmaps

Translating strategic direction into an achievable sequence of change.

I identify the capabilities and dependencies that must be addressed first, structure transition states and establish governance checkpoints so that delivery can proceed without losing sight of the intended outcome.

What you receive: Current and target-state views, transition architectures, dependency analysis, implementation sequence and governance checkpoints.

Specialist expertise

Research-led support for systems under uncertainty

Resilience and Critical Systems Architecture

Understanding how essential services depend on technology, people, processes and governance — and where continuity can break.

I make dependencies, failure modes and recovery considerations visible so that management and assurance specialists can reason about resilience using a coherent evidence base. This is the architectural substance underlying the European NIS2 and CER directives, and in the Netherlands the zorgplicht under the Cyberbeveiligingswet and the designation of kritieke entiteiten under the Wet weerbaarheid kritieke entiteiten.

What you receive: Dependency view, failure-mode analysis, continuity architecture, recovery considerations and a management briefing.

AI Architecture and Governance Readiness

Determining whether the organisation has the information foundations, ownership, controls and monitoring needed to adopt AI responsibly.

The assessment considers data lineage, system accountability, human oversight, model monitoring and the relationship between adaptive systems and existing architecture governance.

What you receive: AI-system and dependency inventory, readiness assessment, governance gaps, data and ownership requirements, and a sequenced improvement roadmap.
