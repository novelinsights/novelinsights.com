Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated: 7 August 2026

Novel Insights takes a deliberately minimal approach to personal data. This site is a brochure site: it uses cookieless, privacy-respecting analytics (GoatCounter) that do not identify individual visitors or store IP addresses, and otherwise has no tracking pixels, no advertising, no cookies that identify you, and no forms that store what you submit.

What this website collects

Nothing that identifies you personally. The site is served through Cloudflare, which processes technical information such as IP address and browser type in order to deliver pages and protect against attacks. Cloudflare acts as a processor on behalf of Novel Insights. The site also uses GoatCounter, a cookieless analytics service, to measure aggregate visits. GoatCounter does not use cookies and does not store IP addresses.

If you contact me

When you send an email or call, I process the contact details and the content of your message so that I can respond and, if we work together, manage the engagement.

  • Lawful basis: legitimate interest in responding to a business enquiry, and where an engagement follows, performance of a contract.
  • Retention: enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted within twelve months. Records relating to an engagement are retained for as long as required by Dutch commercial and tax law.
  • Sharing: your details are not sold, and are not shared with third parties except where necessary to deliver an engagement or where required by law.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to the personal data held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, object to processing, or request that it be transferred. Write to the address below and I will respond within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
