Privacy note

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Novel Insights handles personal data with care and limits its use to operating this website, communicating with you and providing its services.

When you visit this website

This website uses Cloudflare to deliver pages and protect against abuse. This involves processing technical information such as your IP address, browser information and page requests.

GoatCounter provides cookieless visitor statistics to help improve the website. It processes technical information temporarily, including IP addresses, but does not store IP addresses in its analytics database or place cookies on your device.

Fonts are hosted on this website. Novel Insights does not use advertising trackers or sell personal data.

Website delivery, security and limited analytics support Novel Insights’ legitimate interest in maintaining a secure and useful website.

When you contact Novel Insights

If you contact Novel Insights, your contact details and the information you provide are used to respond, discuss potential work and manage any resulting business relationship.

Depending on the circumstances, processing is based on legitimate business interests, steps necessary to enter into or perform a contract, or legal obligations.

Retention and service providers

Personal data is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it is held, including maintaining business relationships, administering engagements and meeting legal obligations.

Novel Insights uses service providers to operate this website and support business communications. These providers process personal data where needed to deliver their services.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data. Depending on the circumstances, you may also request restriction or portability, or object to processing. These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions.

To make a request or ask a privacy question, email hello@novelinsights.com. Requests are normally answered within one month. If a permitted extension is needed, you will be informed.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
